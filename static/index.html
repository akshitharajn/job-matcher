Job Matcher

Describe your skills and what you want (and what you never want). It searches current job postings and explains each match.

Checking job sources...

Your text is sent to a third-party LLM (Groq) to be parsed. Don't paste sensitive personal data.

Live jobs come from free public feeds: Arbeitnow, Remote OK and Jobicy. Each result links to the original posting, where you apply. These feeds are mostly software, data and remote roles, and skew towards Europe.
